## US-0201. Record an AI incident and compute its blast radius

- **Persona**: compliance officer
- **Workflow**: [`WF-AIA-003`](../workflows/catalog.md) Record and act on an AI incident
- **Dimension**: `AIA` Semantic and agent intelligence
- **Complexity tier**: 4
- **Jurisdiction**: US federal
- **Tags**: `agent`, `ai-incident`, `blast-radius`, `suspension`

**Preconditions**

- Tenant acme. Assistant version AG-2026-07 gave incorrect leave eligibility guidance in 14 conversations.
- The error is reported on 2026-08-11; the version was deployed on 2026-07-02.
- Trace retention is 90 days, which covers the whole deployment window.

**Story**

> As a compliance officer, I want the agent version to be suspended before it is patched, so that the evidence survives the fix

**Scenario**

The report arrives and the traces are preserved first. The agent version is suspended rather than patched in place, because patching destroys the state the investigation needs. The blast radius is every conversation the version touched between 2026-07-02 and 2026-08-11, which the 90-day retention covers. Fourteen workers received incorrect eligibility guidance; each is contacted and their leave position corrected where they relied on it. The incident's ObligationState stays PENDING until all fourteen reviews are recorded, so the incident cannot be reported as handled while any of them is outstanding.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. **Given** the incident is reported
   **When** the response runs
   **Then** traces are preserved before the version is touched
2. **Given** the agent version is faulty
   **When** the remediation runs
   **Then** it is suspended rather than patched in place, so the investigation has the state it needs
3. **Given** the blast radius is computed
   **When** the traces are queried
   **Then** every conversation the version touched in the deployment window is enumerated
4. **Given** a worker relied on the incorrect guidance
   **When** the correction runs
   **Then** their leave position is corrected and the correction is a governed intent rather than an informal fix
5. **Given** a worker in the radius did not rely on the guidance
   **When** their case is reviewed
   **Then** the review records no correction required rather than the worker being dropped from the affected set

**Data read**

- `agent`: agent version, traces and affected executions
- `leave`: the eligibility positions the guidance affected
- `workflow`: decisions the agent contributed to

**Data written**

- `agent`: the incident record, the suspension and the remediation
- `leave`: corrected positions where workers relied on the guidance

**Authorization and disclosure**

- The fourteen workers receive FULL disclosure of the correction affecting them; the traces are DENIED to managers and FULL to compliance.

**Failure modes and expected refusals**

- the version is patched before the traces are preserved -> evidence is lost and the blast radius cannot be computed
- an affected decision has already had an employment consequence -> the correction runs through the ordinary corrective intent with notice to the worker

**Evidence and ledger facts expected**

- Preserved traces from before the suspension.
- The blast radius: every decision the version touched.
- Per-decision review outcome.

## US-0202. Diagnose drift with an unknown cause and refuse to plan

- **Persona**: IT or access administrator
- **Workflow**: [`WF-REP-001`](../workflows/catalog.md) Diagnose drift and build a bounded repair plan
- **Dimension**: `REP` Reconciliation, repair and operations
- **Complexity tier**: 4
- **Jurisdiction**: US federal
- **Tags**: `repair`, `drift`, `unknown-cause`, `refuse-to-plan`

**Preconditions**

- Tenant acme. A drift finding shows 42 entitlements present in an application but absent from the model.
- The grant provenance for all 42 is empty: no intent, no operation, no reviewer.
- The observation watermark is current.

**Story**

> As an IT or access administrator, I want a repair against an unknown cause to be refused, so that we do not remove 42 grants and watch them come back

**Scenario**

The finding is produced with both sides and their watermarks. Diagnosis is attempted and fails: the 42 entitlements have no grant provenance at all, so how they arrived is unknown. Planning a repair now would remove them, and without knowing the cause they would return. The planning intent is refused with the unknown cause named, and an investigation opens instead. The investigation finds a legacy provisioning script running outside the platform, which is the real finding.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. **Given** the grant provenance is empty for all 42 entitlements
   **When** diagnosis runs
   **Then** the cause is UNKNOWN and the planning intent is refused rather than producing a plan
2. **Given** an investigation opens
   **When** it proceeds
   **Then** the cause is found outside the platform, which is a finding a repair would have hidden
3. **Given** the finding is produced
   **When** the watermarks are inspected
   **Then** both sides carry their watermark so a stale observation cannot manufacture drift
4. **Given** a plan is eventually built
   **When** it is simulated
   **Then** the simulation reports exactly what each action would change before any approval, and it creates no RequestState of its own because a simulation is a read
5. **Given** the investigation names a cause outside the platform
   **When** a repair is later planned
   **Then** the plan includes stopping the external script, and a plan that removes the 42 grants without it is refused again

**Data read**

- `identity`: grant provenance for the 42 entitlements
- `integration`: observations and their watermarks
- `operations`: drift findings, prior repairs and resource ownership

**Data written**

- `operations`: the refused plan and the investigation record

**Authorization and disclosure**

- Operations receives FULL disclosure of the finding and the unknown cause; the application owner is told 42 entitlements are unexplained.

**Failure modes and expected refusals**

- a plan is built against an unknown cause -> the repair removes the symptom and the cause recreates it
- the observation is stale -> the finding reports UNKNOWN rather than drift; comparing a fresh model to a stale observation manufactures findings

**Evidence and ledger facts expected**

- Both watermarks behind the finding.
- The diagnosis with its explicit unknown.
- The investigation record and its eventual cause.

## US-0203. Execute a repair whose state moved since approval

- **Persona**: IT or access administrator
- **Workflow**: [`WF-REP-002`](../workflows/catalog.md) Execute an approved repair and verify the outcome
- **Dimension**: `REP` Reconciliation, repair and operations
- **Complexity tier**: 4
- **Jurisdiction**: US federal
- **Tags**: `repair`, `stale-plan`, `revalidation`, `verification`

**Preconditions**

- Tenant acme. Repair plan RP-1180 was approved on 2026-05-14 to revoke six entitlements.
- Execution is scheduled for 2026-05-18.
- Between approval and execution, two of the six were revoked by an access review.

**Story**

> As an IT or access administrator, I want a plan whose assumptions have moved to abort and replan, so that we do not execute a stale plan and create a second incident

**Scenario**

The revalidation at execution finds that two of the six entitlements no longer exist: an access review revoked them on 2026-05-16. The plan's assumptions have moved. Execution aborts and the plan is regenerated against current state, then reapproved for the remaining four. Executing the stale plan would have produced two failed actions whose failure would look like a repair problem rather than a state change.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. **Given** two entitlements were revoked between approval and execution
   **When** revalidation runs
   **Then** the state no longer matches the plan and execution aborts before any action
2. **Given** the plan is regenerated
   **When** it is reapproved
   **Then** the four remaining actions are approved on a fresh simulation digest
3. **Given** each action executes
   **When** verification runs
   **Then** each effect is verified by reading back, not by the operation's return code
4. **Given** one action succeeds but its read-back disagrees
   **When** the result is recorded
   **Then** ConsistencyState stays PENDING_OBSERVATION and the drift persists, because a return code is not an observation
5. **Given** half the actions verify
   **When** the repair completes
   **Then** the verified half stands, no false rollback occurs, and the rest reopen as a new finding

**Data read**

- `integration`: the target systems for each action
- `operations`: the approved plan, its simulation and current state

**Data written**

- `operations`: the abort record, the regenerated plan and the execution verifications

**Authorization and disclosure**

- Operations receives FULL disclosure of the abort and its reason; the application owner reapproves on the regenerated plan.

**Failure modes and expected refusals**

- the stale plan is executed -> two actions fail for a reason unrelated to the repair, which contaminates the diagnosis
- verification is skipped for speed -> the repair reports success it cannot substantiate

**Evidence and ledger facts expected**

- The revalidation result before execution.
- Per-action read-back verification.
- The residual set where verification failed.

## US-0204. Quarantine a poisoned message class and decide its disposition

- **Persona**: IT or access administrator
- **Workflow**: [`WF-REP-003`](../workflows/catalog.md) Quarantine poisoned work and drain it safely
- **Dimension**: `REP` Reconciliation, repair and operations
- **Complexity tier**: 3
- **Jurisdiction**: US federal
- **Tags**: `repair`, `quarantine`, `poison-class`, `preservation`, `tier-3`

**Preconditions**

- Tenant acme. 1,840 benefits enrollment messages fail with the same signature over four hours on 2026-11-03.
- The queue depth threshold is 500. The failure signature is a schema validation error.
- Behind them, 96 workflow instances are blocked.

**Story**

> As an IT or access administrator, I want a poison class to be quarantined with its payloads preserved, so that the queue clears without the work being lost

**Scenario**

The repeated failures trip the poison detection at 500. The 1,840 messages are quarantined with their payloads and their failure history preserved; discarding them to clear the queue would silently drop 1,840 enrollments. An incident names the class, the count and the blocked instances. Diagnosis finds a carrier schema change. The disposition is transform and replay: the payloads are mapped to the new schema and replayed. Discarding would have required approval, because it is the only path that loses work.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. **Given** the same failure signature repeats past the threshold
   **When** detection fires
   **Then** the class is quarantined with payloads and failure history preserved rather than discarded
2. **Given** 96 instances are blocked behind the class
   **When** the incident is raised
   **Then** it names the class, the count and the blocked instances rather than reporting a generic queue depth alert
3. **Given** the disposition is transform and replay
   **When** the drain runs
   **Then** the payloads are mapped and replayed, and the outcomes are recorded per item
4. **Given** a discard is proposed instead
   **When** the approval resolves
   **Then** discard requires approval; it is the only path that loses work
5. **Given** a replayed item fails identically
   **When** it is evaluated
   **Then** it returns to quarantine with an incremented attempt count and the class disposition is revisited

**Data read**

- `operations`: queue state, failure signatures and prior quarantines
- `workflow`: the instances blocked behind the poisoned items

**Data written**

- `operations`: quarantine records and the drain dispositions

**Authorization and disclosure**

- Operations receives FULL disclosure of the class and its payloads; the affected workers are not contacted until the disposition is decided.

**Failure modes and expected refusals**

- items are discarded to clear the queue -> REJECT without approval; silently dropping work is how a payroll batch loses a worker
- the quarantine grows faster than it drains -> escalate; the upstream must be stopped rather than the detection tuned

**Evidence and ledger facts expected**

- Preserved payloads and their failure history.
- The per-class disposition with its reason.
- The approval for any discard.

## US-0205. Run a recovery test that misses its objective

- **Persona**: IT or access administrator
- **Workflow**: [`WF-REP-004`](../workflows/catalog.md) Run a recovery test and prove the restore works
- **Dimension**: `REP` Reconciliation, repair and operations
- **Complexity tier**: 4
- **Jurisdiction**: US federal
- **Tags**: `repair`, `recovery-test`, `objective-miss`, `isolation`

**Preconditions**

- Tenant acme. The recovery contract states a four-hour objective time and a fifteen-minute objective point.
- A quarterly restore test on 2026-04-18 completes in five hours and twenty minutes.
- The restored ledger chain verifies intact.

**Story**

> As an IT or access administrator, I want a test that exceeds its objective to be recorded as a failure, so that the contract means something rather than being aspirational

**Scenario**

The backup set is restored into an isolated environment. Content verification passes: the ledger chain is intact, row counts match, referential integrity holds and no tenant leak is present. The measured recovery time is five hours twenty minutes against a four-hour objective. That is a failed test, not a passed test with a note: a restore that took longer than the objective did not meet the contract. The measured figures are recorded rather than a pass or fail alone, and the restored environment is destroyed.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. **Given** the restore completes in five hours twenty minutes
   **When** the measurement is compared
   **Then** it exceeds the four-hour objective and the test's BusinessState is FAILED, which is a result rather than an absence of one
2. **Given** the content verification passes
   **When** the result is recorded
   **Then** content integrity and time objective are recorded as separate results rather than one verdict
3. **Given** the ledger chain were broken
   **When** verification runs
   **Then** the test fails regardless of timing; a restored database that cannot prove its own integrity is not a recovery
4. **Given** the restored environment holds production data
   **When** the test completes
   **Then** it is destroyed and the destruction is recorded, because a restore is a copy carrying every obligation of the original
5. **Given** the test is skipped because production is stable
   **When** the cadence is evaluated
   **Then** the skip is a finding; an untested backup is a hypothesis

**Data read**

- `ledger`: chain integrity for verification
- `operations`: backup sets, the recovery contract and prior test results

**Data written**

- `operations`: the restore test record with its measured objectives and findings

**Authorization and disclosure**

- Operations and the risk owner receive FULL disclosure of the measured figures; the restored data is accessible only to the test operators and only during the test.

**Failure modes and expected refusals**

- the result is recorded as a pass with a note about timing -> the contract's objective becomes decorative
- production data is restored into an environment with wider access -> an immediate incident; the copy inherits every obligation of the original

**Evidence and ledger facts expected**

- Measured recovery time and point.
- Content verification findings including tenant isolation.
- The destruction record for the restored environment.

> As a finance partner, I want every invoice line to trace to the intents behind it, so that a disputed charge can be answered with evidence

The entitlement is applied before billing rather than as a credit afterwards, so the invoice shows only the overage. Each line traces to the specific intents behind it. 3. **Given** a line is queried
   **When** the trace is produced
   **Then** it resolves to the specific intents behind the line
4. **Given** a line cannot be traced

**Data written**

- `billing`: rated usage, the invoice and its lines

## US-0207. Resolve a billing dispute with a partial credit

- Tenant acme. Customer borealis disputes three lines on the 2026-08 invoice totalling 2,140.00 USD.

**Story**

> As an external partner or carrier, I want a partial credit to be recorded as partial rather than as a full one, so that the dispute history means something for later analysis

**Scenario**

The three lines are examined against their usage traces. Two are supported and are upheld with the trace produced to the customer. One includes 380.00 USD of sandbox usage that the contract excludes from billing, which is a metering defect rather than a disagreement.    **When** the credit is issued
   **Then** it is an adjustment document referencing the original invoice, and the invoice is not edited
3. **Given** the credit is 380.00 USD against a 1,000.00 USD threshold
   **When** the approval resolves
   **Then** a single approver suffices, the threshold applied is recorded, and RequestState reaches APPROVED on that one decision
4. **Given** the same line type is disputed again next month
   **When** the pattern is evaluated
   **Then** the metering defect finding is escalated; a recurring dispute means the metering or the contract is wrong

**Data read**

- `billing`: the invoice, its lines, the usage events and prior disputes

**Data written**

- `billing`: the dispute record and the adjustment document
- `operations`: the metering defect finding

**Authorization and disclosure**

- The customer receives FULL disclosure of the usage trace behind each upheld line and of the 380.00 USD adjustment; the metering defect finding is WITHHELD, because the customer is told the credit is owed without being told the platform mismetered.

**Failure modes and expected refusals**

- the original invoice is edited -> REJECT; an amended invoice with no trace is an audit finding and a revenue recognition problem
- the dispute is raised after the window -> the window is enforced but the underlying question is still answered

**Evidence and ledger facts expected**

- Line detail produced to the customer.
- The adjustment approval at the right threshold.
- The original invoice, unaltered.
