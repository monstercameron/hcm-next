# Alabama Employment Law

## 1. Summary for HCM Next

- Alabama is a strict at-will employment state with very limited statutory exceptions; termination without cause is permitted.
- No state minimum wage, overtime, or mandated pay frequency laws; federal FLSA applies.
- Clarke-Figures Equal Pay Act (Ala. Code § 25-1-30, effective Sept 1, 2019) prohibits wage discrimination based on sex or race for substantially similar work; requires 3-year wage record retention.
- No state mandate for paid sick leave, family leave, or personal leave in private sector; federal FMLA applies to eligible employers.
- Overtime now fully subject to state income tax as of July 1, 2025 (Act 2024-382 tax exemption expired).
- Workers' compensation retaliation exception (Ala. Code § 25-5-11) prohibits termination "solely because" of workers' compensation claim, using strict standard.
- Adopted IRS Common Law Right-of-Control Test (Ala. Act 2021-226) for independent contractor classification across wage/hour, unemployment, and income tax.
- Non-compete agreements enforceable if consideration exists (≤2 years presumed reasonable); apply only in specific limited contexts.
- Federal WARN Act applies to employers with 100+ employees in Alabama.
- Right-to-work state (Ala. Code Title 25, Ch. 7, Art. 2); union membership and dues cannot be required as condition of employment.

## 2. Employment Relationship

**At-Will Employment Status:**
Alabama law establishes at-will employment as the default relationship. Employers may terminate employees with or without cause, with limited statutory exceptions.

**Statutory Exceptions to At-Will:**

- **Workers' Compensation Retaliation Protection (Ala. Code § 25-5-11.01):** Employers are prohibited from terminating an employee "solely because" the employee files a workers' compensation claim or reports written safety rule violations in writing. Note: Alabama courts apply a strict "solely because" standard; mixed-motive discharges typically fail this exception.
- **Jury Duty Protection (Ala. Code § 12-16-8):** Employers must excuse full-time employees summoned for jury duty without requiring vacation or sick leave use. Employees receive usual compensation. Small employer exception: Courts may postpone jury duty for employees of firms with ≤5 full-time employees if another employee also summoned.
- **Federal Protections:** Title VII (race, color, religion, sex, national origin), ADA (disability), ADEA (age 40+), GINA (genetic information), USERRA (military service).

**Public Policy Exception:**
No state public policy exception exists in Alabama. Courts have explicitly rejected a generalized exception; only legislatively enacted exceptions apply.

**Required Written Notices:**
No state law requires written notice of hire, termination, or pay/role change terms at hire. Federal FLSA applies: employers must inform employees of rates and pay periods.

**Wage Theft Prevention:**
No state wage theft prevention notice requirement. Federal FLSA notice applies.

## 3. Wages

**Minimum Wage:**
No state minimum wage statute; federal FLSA minimum of $7.25/hour applies.

**Overtime:**
No state overtime statute; federal FLSA 1.5x overtime for hours exceeding 40/week applies (minimum $10.88/hour). **Change (2025):** Overtime income is now fully subject to Alabama state income tax as of July 1, 2025; the overtime tax exemption (Act 2024-382) expired June 30, 2025.

**Pay Frequency:**
No state-mandated pay frequency law. Employers may pay weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, or monthly. Federal FLSA default applies: final paycheck paid on next regularly scheduled payday.

**Final Paycheck on Termination:**
No state final paycheck statute. Employees paid on next regularly scheduled payday regardless of separation. **Commission Exception:** Commissions due at termination must be paid within 30 days of termination; commissions becoming due after termination paid within 30 days of becoming due. (verify exact statute citation: approx. Ala. Code § 34-10-1 and related provisions in sales representative chapter)

**Permitted Deductions:**
No state-specific statute governing permitted deductions beyond federal FLSA rules. Employers must obtain employee consent for deductions in writing; deductions cannot reduce pay below minimum wage.

**Pay Statement Content:**
No state pay statement content requirements. Federal FLSA applies: employees must be able to verify wages earned and hours worked.

## 4. Pay Transparency and Equity

**Clarke-Figures Equal Pay Act (Ala. Code § 25-1-30, effective Sept 1, 2019):**
Prohibits wage rate discrimination based on sex or race for "substantially similar work" when viewed as a composite of skill, effort, responsibility, and similar working conditions. Applies to public and private employers. Requires employers to retain records of wages, wage rates, job classifications, and employment terms and conditions for 3 years.

**Salary History Bans:**
No state law restricts use of salary history in hiring decisions. Employers may ask applicants about prior salary; no ban exists.

**Wage Transparency in Postings:**
No state requirement to post or disclose salary ranges in job postings or upon request. Federal only.

**Pay Data Reporting:**
No state pay equity audit or pay data reporting requirement. Federal EEO-1 applies (employers with 100+ employees).

## 5. Leave and Time

**Paid Sick Leave (Private Sector):**
No state mandate for private sector paid sick leave. Employers free to establish own policies. Exception: State employees receive 4 hours sick leave per biweekly service period (public sector only).

**Family Leave:**
Federal FMLA applies (employees at employers with 50+ employees within 75-mile radius, 12+ months employment, 1,250+ hours worked in prior year). 12 weeks unpaid, job-protected. No state FMLA.

**Paid Parental Leave (Public Sector):**
Alabama Public Employee Paid Parental Leave Act (effective July 1, 2025): Up to 8 weeks paid parental leave for mothers; up to 2 weeks paid parental leave for fathers or adoption. Private sector: no state requirement.

**Personal Leave:**
No state mandate for personal leave days. Federal jury duty exemption applies (Ala. Code § 12-16-8).

**Military Service Leave:**
Federal USERRA applies: up to 5 years unpaid military leave, reemployment in same or comparable job. Alabama National Guard State Duty Exception: Same USERRA protections apply for state active duty ≥30 consecutive days or federally funded duty.

**Interaction with Pay or Role Changes:**
Leave usage is controlled by employer policy and federal law. Promotion or pay change does not reset leave accrual unless policy explicitly provides. No state law addresses interaction.

## 6. Records and Access

**Payroll Record Retention:**
Minimum 3-year retention of wages, pay periods, hours worked, job titles, classifications, pay rates, and employment terms and conditions (required by equal pay law, Ala. Code § 25-1-30).

**Personnel File Access:**
No state law grants employees right to inspect personnel files. Federal privacy law applies. (verify Alabama code for any personnel privacy statute)

**Unemployment Records:**
Minimum 5-year retention.

**Workers' Compensation Records:**
Minimum 12-year retention for all injury records (fatal and non-fatal) where payment claimed or made.

**Record Format:**
No state requirement for electronic format; paper or electronic acceptable.

## 7. Privacy and Data

**State Privacy Law:**
Alabama Personal Data Protection Act (APDPA, 2024) applies to consumer personal data broadly but **excludes employee data**. Applies only to breach notification: 45-day notification required if breach exposes biometric data with name. Limited enforcement for employee data.

**Biometric Data:**
No state biometric privacy law restricting employer collection. Employers may collect fingerprints, facial recognition, and other biometric data without consent or notice. Federal only.

**Workplace Monitoring:**
No state electronic monitoring or surveillance law. Federal baseline applies. (verify: check for any camera/audio recording restrictions)

**Breach Notification:**
APDPA 45-day notification if biometric data with name exposed in breach. No notification requirement for other employee data categories in Alabama.

**Data Residency or Processing Constraints:**
No state requirement. Federal compliance applies.

## 8. Hiring and Background

**Ban-the-Box:**
No statewide ban-the-box law for private employers. Local exception: City of Birmingham (2016 executive order) applies to city government jobs only, not private employers.

**Background Checks:**
Governed by federal FCRA only. No state mini-FCRA or additional restrictions.

**Drug Testing:**
Permitted. Employers may establish drug testing and drug-free workplace policies. Employers may prohibit cannabis use and enforce through testing. Drug-Free Workplace Program offers 5% workers' compensation insurance discount; requires written policy and post-offer, reasonable suspicion, fitness-for-duty, post-accident, and follow-up testing provisions.

**E-Verify:**
No state mandate for private employers. Federal I-9 verification applies.

**Credit Checks:**
No state restrictions on employer credit report use.

## 9. Separation

**Severance:**
No state severance requirement. Severance may be required by contract or collective bargaining agreement. (Note: May be needed for enforceability of non-compete with existing employees in some Alabama circuits.)

**Non-Compete Agreements:**
Governed by Ala. Code § 8-1-190(a). General rule: contracts restraining lawful profession, trade, or business are void. Narrow exceptions permit non-competes in specific circumstances. For existing employees (not new hires), independent consideration beyond continued employment is typically required in most Alabama circuits. Presumptions of reasonableness: employee non-competes ≤2 years presumed reasonable; business sale non-competes ≤1 year presumed reasonable. (verify: confirm whether 2025-2026 amendments expand or restrict non-compete enforceability)

**Non-Solicit:**
No separate state non-solicit statute. General contract law applies; enforceability varies by circuit.

**Mini-WARN Act:**
No state mini-WARN. Federal WARN Act applies: employers with 100+ employees must provide 60-day advance notice of mass layoffs (50+ workers) or plant closings to affected employees, state workforce agency (Alabama Department of Labor), and chief elected local official. Notice must include expected separation date and schedule. Back pay and benefits owed for missed notice period.

## 10. Classification and Multi-State

**Independent Contractor Test:**
Alabama Act 2021-226 (2021) adopted the **IRS Common Law "Right of Control" Test** (20-factor analysis) for wage/hour, unemployment, and income tax purposes. Does not apply to workers' compensation classification, which uses traditional right-of-control standard (Ala. Code Title 25, Ch. 5).

**Three Prongs of Common Law Test:**

1. Behavioral control: Does company control how worker performs tasks?
2. Financial control: Worker's investment, profit/loss potential, multiple clients?
3. Relationship factors: How do parties view the relationship?

**Safe Harbor:**
Section 530 protection available if employer had reasonable basis for IC classification, issued 1099s, and history of consistent IC treatment.

**Remote Work:**
No state-specific telecommuting law. Tax benefit: Nonresident employees working <30 days in Alabama exempt from state income tax withholding (2026 change). ADA may require remote work as reasonable accommodation. No telecommuting tax credit.

**Multi-State Worker Implications:**
Businesses in multiple states must comply with conflicting classification tests (Alabama common law vs. ABC test in other states). Platform must flag when same worker crosses state lines.

## 11. Implications for P1A/P1B

**Must Model as Typed Obligation or Notice:**

- **Equal Pay Audit Trail:** Capture job classifications, skills, effort, responsibility, and working conditions for each promotion or pay change; enable proof of "substantially similar work" evaluation (Ala. Code § 25-1-30).
- **Wage Record Retention:** Automatically retain all wage, pay rate, job classification, and term/condition records for 3-year minimum; flag records older than 3 years for archive review.
- **Workers' Compensation Termination Restriction:** Flag if termination reason is workers' compensation claim or safety report; block or require manager acknowledgment if "solely because" reason detected.
- **Non-Compete Enforceability Check:** For existing-employee non-compete enforcement, confirm independent consideration has been documented (beyond mere continued employment).
- **Classification Audit:** Document the 20-factor right-of-control analysis for any contractor reclassification; flag if historical 1099 treatment conflicts with new classification.

**Must Model as Timing Constraint:**

- **Commission Payment:** 30-day payment deadline for commissions earned at termination or thereafter.
- **Overtime Tax Withholding:** As of July 1, 2025, overtime income subject to state income tax; update payroll withholding tables.

**Must Model as Field Restriction:**

- **At-Will Acknowledgment:** Require manager to confirm Alabama at-will employment applies (except jury duty, workers' compensation retaliation).
- **Right-to-Work Compliance:** Prohibit any employer action conditioning employment on union membership or dues payment.
