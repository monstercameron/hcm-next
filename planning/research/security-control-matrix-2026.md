Source: `research/security-best-practices-2026.md` (read date 2026-09-05), cross-referenced against
the live `planning/todos.md` backlog (TRUST, AUTHN, ANON, PRIV, CRYPTO, SUPPLY, TOOL-01x, CICD, LIB,
GOV, DB-017, TENANT sections) and the packages named in that research: `internal/trust` and its
subpackages, `internal/authn` and `issuerregistry`, `internal/domains/pseudonym`,

`internal/data/tenancy`, the RLS migration (`migrations/00008_tenant_isolation.sql`), and
`internal/transport/{envelope,edge,grpcserver}`.

Status legend: **IMPLEMENTED** — a shipped, tested package or migration satisfies the control today.
**PARTIAL** — a real mechanism exists but a named gap remains open. **MISSING** — no implementation
and no shipped mechanism exists in the named packages. A cited todo ID that is unchecked (`[ ]`) is
scheduled work, not evidence of completion; only `[x]` todos with an Evidence line are cited as
IMPLEMENTED support. New todos added to `planning/todos.md` on 2026-09-05 are cited as `NEW:<ID>`.
