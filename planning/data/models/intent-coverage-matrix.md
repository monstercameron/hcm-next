field-level read/write/effect sets. No intent in a partition may introduce an

## DataOps additions

The canonical DataOps definitions are in
[DataOps, Schema, Reference and Configuration Entities](dataops-configuration.md).
They are not repeated here so the matrix cannot become a competing entity
registry.

## Coverage assertion

All 530 intent names fit one or more rows above without requiring a new universal
kernel family. Coverage does not mean domain depth is complete. Each future
IntentDefinition must prove its exact entity/property dependencies, and CI should
reject definitions referencing unknown or ownerless entity types.
